# Contributing · 参与贡献

Useful contributions show a specific writing problem and a better decision.

欢迎提交能帮助识别具体写作问题、改进判断方法的贡献。

## Report a case · 提交案例

Open an [issue](https://github.com/git-zyyang/anti-defensive-writing/issues) with:

1. The task, language, reader, and genre. / 任务、语言、读者和文体。
2. A short passage you have permission to share. Label constructed examples. / 有权公开的短段落；虚构教学示例须标明。
3. The relevant facts and constraints. / 已知事实与限制。
4. The actual problem and its effect on meaning or reader experience. / 具体问题及其对含义或体验的影响。
5. A proposed edit, including what must be preserved. / 建议改法和必须保护的内容。

Remove private information before posting. Summarize unpublished work with a constructed example rather than uploading the manuscript.

发布前移除私人信息。涉及未公开稿件时，用教学案例说明问题。

## Propose a change · 提交修改

Keep the change focused. Update the English and Chinese README when user-facing behavior changes, and update affected references and examples together. Preserve license notices and provenance.

修改围绕具体问题展开。行为变化同步更新双语说明、相关方法与示例，并保留许可和来源。

Please avoid universal word bans, mandatory shortening quotas, invented evidence, or rules that turn one author's preference into a requirement for every genre.

请避免将特定词语、篇幅或单个作者偏好变成所有文体的硬性规则。

## Review checklist · 检查方式

- Does the change solve the reported problem? / 是否解决已报告的问题？
- Does it preserve evidence, necessary conditions, and voice? / 是否保留证据、必要条件与声音？
- Does it respect the requested editing scope? / 是否遵守修改范围？
- Are relative links and installation instructions still valid? / 相对链接和安装说明是否有效？

Structural checks and model-generated examples do not establish writing quality by themselves. When reporting a behavioral evaluation, include the task, input, model/runtime, observed output, and the basis for judging improvement. Keep historical regression cases separate from genuinely new evaluation cases.
