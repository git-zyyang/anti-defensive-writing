## Inherited foundations

- Functional judgment of disclaimers and qualifications.
- Direct presentation of the supported claim and scope.
- Calibration of hedging to evidence.
- Preservation of methodological conditions and adverse results.
- Separation of reader-facing prose from internal review discussion.

## Development in this adaptation

The local adaptation developed through the maintainer's writing workflow and feedback. It extends the method to idea development, substantive revision, theoretical depth, creative experience, evaluation of review comments, and checks for value lost through editing.

The public package translates and generalizes the adapted entry, includes a portable expression guide, and uses freshly written bilingual teaching examples. Private feedback transcripts, project manuscripts, personal profiles, local paths, and internal conversation identifiers are excluded.

公开版将本地入口翻译、通用化，纳入可独立使用的表达方法与新编双语教学案例。构思推进、审稿裁决、理论与声音保护等扩展归于本适配版本，不归为上游作者原文。私人记录不随项目发布。

The expression guide also incorporates local functional checks associated with the maintainer's lieflat adaptation: identifiable referents, meaningful sentence variation, justified contrast, and concrete support for evaluations. No external corpus statistics or universal effectiveness claims are carried over.

## Maintenance

Compare upstream changes before incorporating them. Preserve attribution and the adapted method's supported distinctions. Publishing a new version requires checking the actual instructions, references, and examples together. Ordinary use of the skill does not trigger updates or publication.
