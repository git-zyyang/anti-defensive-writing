# Bilingual teaching examples · 双语教学案例

All cases below are constructed illustrations. They are not private drafts, empirical findings, benchmark results, or author-approved output. Their stated premises authorize the content of the example only.

以下均为教学示例。案例中的已知条件仅用于展示判断方法，不作为真实研究发现或模型效果数据。具体措辞可以不同。

## 1. Develop the mechanism · 展开机制

**Given / 已知**：A shared glossary gives two teams matching definitions and reduces clarification exchanges. No evidence of faster final delivery is available. / 共享词表统一两组团队的术语定义，减少澄清往返；没有最终交付速度的证据。

**Before / 原稿**

> This small change may be helpful, although it cannot solve every coordination problem.<br>
> 这一小改动或许有所帮助，但不能解决全部协作问题。

**After / 改写**

> The shared glossary gives both teams the same definitions, reducing the back-and-forth needed to clarify terms.<br>
> 共享词表使两组团队按相同定义理解术语，减少了反复澄清含义的往返沟通。

**Why / 判断**：Develop the supported relationship. Do not add a delivery-speed claim. / 把已有材料支持的关系写出来，不新增交付提速结论。

## 2. Keep association as association · 保留相关性

**Given / 已知**：An observational analysis finds a positive association; there is no causal identification. / 观察性分析发现正相关，未做因果识别。

**Before / 原稿**

> This may possibly suggest that X could be positively associated with Y.<br>
> 这或许可能在一定程度上提示，X可能与Y正相关。

**After / 改写**

> In this observational analysis, X is positively associated with Y.<br>
> 在该观察性分析中，X与Y呈正相关。

**Keep / 保留**：The observational scope. If the direction or estimate is itself uncertain, describe that uncertainty. / 保留观察性口径；若方向或估计本身不确定，仍须表达不确定性。

## 3. Distinguish plans from achievements · 区分计划与成果

**Given / 已知**：The team completed an interview guide and plans to interview managers. / 团队已完成访谈提纲，计划访谈管理者。

**Before / 原稿**

> We have only prepared an interview guide and can at most make a preliminary attempt to interview managers.<br>
> 我们仅准备了访谈提纲，至多只能初步尝试访谈管理者。

**After / 改写**

> We have completed the interview guide and plan to interview managers.<br>
> 我们已完成访谈提纲，拟访谈管理者。

**Keep / 保留**：The interviews remain planned. If “preliminary” names a real pilot phase, retain it. / 访谈仍是计划；“初步”若指真实预访谈阶段，应保留。

## 4. Report the full tradeoff · 完整呈现权衡

**Given / 已知**：Under the same test conditions, latency falls from 100 ms to 60 ms and accuracy falls from 90% to 89%. / 同一测试条件下，延迟由100毫秒降至60毫秒，准确率由90%降至89%。

**Before / 原稿**

> Performance improved substantially; the slight accuracy loss should not detract from this success.<br>
> 性能大幅改善，轻微的准确率损失不应影响对成果的肯定。

**After / 改写**

> Under the same test conditions, latency fell from 100 ms to 60 ms, while accuracy fell from 90% to 89%.<br>
> 同一测试条件下，延迟从100毫秒降至60毫秒，准确率从90%降至89%。

**Keep / 保留**：Both changes and the comparison conditions. The acceptability of the tradeoff requires a use case. / 两项变化及比较条件都保留，是否值得取决于实际用途。

## 5. Preserve a consequential limitation · 保留影响理解的限制

> The sample covers three pilot cities and cannot support a national prevalence estimate.<br>
> 样本覆盖三座试点城市，不能据此估计全国普及率。

**Decision / 处理**：Keep this if readers might interpret the result as a national estimate. A standalone abstract may need it even if methods already explain the sample. / 若读者可能将结果理解为全国估计，就保留；摘要独立传播时可以重述必要限制。

## 6. Preserve a real distinction · 保留真实辨析

> Access to a dataset does not establish ownership of it.<br>
> 获得数据集的访问权限，并不意味着拥有该数据集。

**Decision / 处理**：Keep the distinction when it matters to the task; verify the relevant rights in a real application. Negation has a substantive job here. / 区分与任务有关时保留，实际权利关系另行核验；此处否定承担具体功能。

## 7. Preserve hesitation and voice · 保留犹疑与声音

> “I'm not asking you to stay.” He left the porch light on.<br>
> “我没说要你留下。”他没有关门廊的灯。

**Decision / 处理**：In a fictional scene, contradiction between speech and action can carry feeling. Do not replace it with a report that the character is “emotionally conflicted.” / 在虚构场景中，话语与动作的反差可以承载情绪，不必改成人物“内心矛盾”的说明。

## 8. Preserve responsibility · 保留责任

> Sorry, I left the attachment out. I've attached it now.<br>
> 抱歉，刚才漏了附件，现已补上。

**Decision / 处理**：Keep the apology when it acknowledges a real mistake. The attachment must actually have been added; drafting a message does not send it. / 真实疏漏需要承认；“已补上”须符合实际状态，写好消息不等于已发送。

## 9. Evaluate a proposal to narrow the research · 判断收窄建议

**Given / 已知**：An earlier study examines adoption. The new question concerns how adoption changes coordination. / 既有研究讨论采用行为，新问题讨论采用之后的协作变化。

**Unhelpful response / 无助于推进的回应**

> Adoption has already been studied, so you must choose a smaller setting.<br>
> 采用行为已经有人研究，因此必须缩小研究范围。

**Useful response / 可用回应**

> Compare the earlier study's explanation of adoption with the proposed explanation of post-adoption coordination. Identify the additional relationship and the evidence needed to investigate it.<br>
> 对照前人对采用行为的解释与当前对采用后协作变化的解释，明确新增的关系及其所需证据。

**Keep / 保留**：This is a direction for investigation, not a verified novelty claim. / 这是可推进的研究方向，不是已经核实的新颖性结论。

## 10. Respect read-only work · 尊重只读范围

**Request / 请求**：“Identify the problems; do not rewrite or edit the file.” / “只指出问题，不改写、不改文件。”

**Response / 处理**：Locate the repeated caveat or unsupported claim, explain its function and consequence, and describe a possible repair. Leave the file and quotations unchanged. / 定位重复限定或无依据主张，说明作用、影响与可选改法，保持文件和引语原状。
