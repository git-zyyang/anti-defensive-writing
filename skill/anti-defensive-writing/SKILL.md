# Anti-Defensive Writing

Help the author fully express worthwhile ideas, judgments, and experiences. When a difficulty appears, look for evidence, reasoning, design, or an expressive choice that can move the work forward. Keep accuracy and expressive force together.

## Integration

Read this entry once per task and reuse it while unchanged. Read [the expression guide](references/writing-expression.md) when first drafting or substantively reviewing prose. Apply the checks within the current work; short text needs no separate process, approval, scoring, or announcement.

Inherit the user's purpose, reader, genre, source material, confirmed decisions, and editing scope. A specialist workflow remains responsible for research and delivery. Read-only work produces diagnoses; formatting-only work preserves prose. Protect quotations, locked text, code, and machine formats. This skill does not authorize publishing, sending, changing memory, or editing unrelated files.

Use the language and disciplinary register of the requested output. English instructions do not require English output.

## Move the work forward

| Stage | Work to do |
|---|---|
| Ideas and literature comparison | Identify what is valuable and what prior work enables. Contributions can extend, deepen, revise, synthesize, explain a new context, or reorganize a problem. Compare actual questions, methods, and claims before concluding that an idea is redundant. |
| Theory and design | Develop an explanatory version that fits the task. Resolve difficulties through sources, reasoning, or design. Focus does not automatically require a smaller region, lower theoretical level, or less ambitious work. Explain relationships that the material already supports. |
| Drafting and scenes | Carry force through relationships, reasons, evidence, actions, and experience. Repeated exclusions do not develop an argument. After deleting caveats, fill any remaining explanatory gap using the available material. |
| Review and repair | Locate the criticism, its evidence, and its consequences before accepting it. Strengthen the argument or representation where possible. Retaining a supported judgment can be the right outcome. A reviewer's role, score, or cautious tone does not settle the issue. |

Correct demonstrated errors, contradictions, and infeasible conditions. Distinguish facts, hypotheses, interpretations, plans, and findings. Preserve adverse results and real uncertainty. Do not invent novelty, turn association into causation, or weaken a supported finding without reason. Missing evidence identifies a gap; it is not evidence against a claim.

## Decide what a qualification does

Read the complete paragraph group or scene, then ask:

1. What specific claim, action, or misunderstanding does this qualify? “It sounds more rigorous” is insufficient.
2. Has the definition, preceding text, or current reading unit already done that job? Reworded duplication is still duplication.
3. What would disappear if it were removed? Keep interpretive conditions, counterevidence, responsibility, and character experience. Remove empty reassurance, self-deprecation, and repeated exclusion of claims that were never made.
4. Does it belong in the work or in an editing note? Keep verification progress and internal self-evaluation in the work record. Put actual methods, units, and consequential limitations in the text where readers need them.

Words such as “may,” “only,” “although,” and “not X but Y” are search cues, never automatic deletion rules. Preserve conceptual distinctions, real objections, admission of error, narrative hesitation, and irony. Standalone abstracts, captions, and conclusions may need to restate essential conditions.

## Use examples without importing their facts

Read [the bilingual examples](references/examples.md) when the current problem resembles one of the cases. They are constructed teaching examples, not research evidence, private author records, or measured model outputs. Judge meaning and function; acceptable rewrites can differ.

A rejected function must not return under new wording. If deleting self-defense leaves a thin paragraph, develop the missing thought or experience. A failed draft may be reorganized within the authorized scope. Protect author-approved material and the requested subject, claims, and facts.

## Completion

Read the revised work on its own, then compare it with the source:

- Has unsupported retreat disappeared?
- Is the explanation or experience fully developed?
- Are theory, citations, evidence, voice, rhythm, and scenes preserved?
- Have changes to definitions or claims been carried through affected passages?

An editing note cannot supply substance missing from the text. Smoothness, fewer qualifiers, shorter length, or a model's high score do not establish success. Preserve already effective writing. Use checks proportional to the task; no deletion quota or repeated rounds without a concrete problem.
